## Recent important decisions

- **OxiDJ is MIT-licensed (ADR-009) — decided by the project owner.** `LICENSE` created; copyright holder Senad Ličina. This was not bookkeeping: it fired the conditional constraint in ADR-001, so **Slint is excluded at its free tier and egui/eframe becomes the framework to prototype**, and it excludes **Rubber Band** outright (already rejected independently — 208 lifetime downloads on its Rust bindings). Consequence now required: a **permissive-only dependency policy enforced as a build gate** — `cargo deny check licenses` with an allowlist (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2/3-Clause, ISC, Unicode-DFS-2016, Zlib) and GPL/LGPL/AGPL/SSPL denied by default, wired into `M0-08`. An accidental copyleft dependency would silently change the licence of the distributed binary, so this is a gate rather than a convention. Noted but not acted on: Rust convention often duals `MIT OR Apache-2.0` (Apache adds a patent grant); trivially changeable now, needs contributor agreement later.
- **Audio engine foundation set, but as a candidate (ADR-008).** CPAL for device I/O with the time-stretch engine behind a replaceable trait, Signalsmith Stretch first — **medium** confidence, because *"CPAL is a reasonable Rust-first default, not proof of DJ-grade performance."* The decision's durable output is the **M0 data boundary**: stable track identities, source-frame positions, versioned beat-grid/analysis units, an explicit shared engine-frame clock, bounded timestamped commands, preallocated PCM transfer, defined underrun behaviour, and off-callback decoding plus device recovery. `rubberband` is ruled out on two independent grounds — GPL-2.0+/commercial licence **and** only 208 lifetime downloads, so its Rust bindings are effectively unproven. Verified crate health: `cpal` 24.3M downloads / `rubato` 13.2M / `cubeb` 526k / `signalsmith-stretch` 117k.
- **UI prototype confirmed; accessibility is the gating risk (ADR-001).** Prototype egui, compare Slint, **defer Qt rather than disqualify it** — Qt's maturity does not transfer to `cxx-qt`, which its own maintainers describe as pre-stable with frequently changing APIs, and the announced Qt Bridges may force further change. Slint's GPL/commercial terms are a *conditional* constraint rather than a veto. The prototype gate is explicitly **not a scrolling benchmark**: screen-reader table navigation, keyboard multi-selection and drag-and-drop must pass alongside the 500k-row performance target.
